PROJECT 2 – KEY FINDINGS

1. 1,200 orders were analyzed from January 1, 2023 to June 30, 2025.
2. Average order value was 1,053.97, compared with a median of 823.62, indicating influence from higher-value orders.
3. Chair generated the highest total revenue (195,620.11), narrowly ahead of Printer (195,612.61).
4. Laptop had the highest average order value (1,110.56).
5. Credit Card had the highest revenue (263,847.63) and highest average order value (1,127.55).
6. Instagram generated the highest order volume (259) and highest referral revenue (275,285.45).
7. Facebook had the highest referral-source average order value (1,098.29).
8. FREESHIP had the highest order count (313) and revenue (335,036.99) among coupon categories.
9. Cancelled orders had the highest associated revenue (276,396.21) and average order value (1,105.58), requiring investigation.
10. Eight high-value TotalPrice outliers were detected using IQR. They were retained because their values are consistent with Quantity × UnitPrice.
11. UnitPrice had the strongest correlation with TotalPrice (0.717).
12. Quantity had a positive correlation with TotalPrice (0.615).
13. Quantity and UnitPrice were almost uncorrelated (0.015).

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION NOTE:
Correlation and observed associations do not establish causation. Cancelled order value should not be described as lost revenue because refund/payment settlement data is unavailable.
